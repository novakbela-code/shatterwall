The wall is coming. You have one shot per round.

ShatterWall is a turn-based brick breaker built for VR. Line up one perfect shot, release a stream of up to 60 balls and watch them ricochet through a neon arena. Break the advancing blocks before they reach the red line.

  • Meta Quest 2, 3 and 3S
  • Single player
  • Motion controllers required

How a round plays

  1. Aim

    Point your controller and follow the glowing line as it bounces off the walls. Lock the aim and fine-tune it with your wrist, or fly into the arena to check the angles.

  2. Release

    Every ball you own leaves along the line you aimed. You start with one and hit the +1 pickups to build the stream up to 60.

  3. Shatter

    The number on a block shows how much it can still take. Set off chain reactions with explosive blocks, bombs, row bombs, shooter blocks and the Blast Ball.

  4. Hold the line

    After each round the blocks move one step closer. If one reaches the red line, the run is over.

In the game

Endless runs
Generated layers get tougher as you go. Beat your best round and volley record.
Replays
Runs are recorded automatically on your headset. Change the viewpoint, slow down, freeze, save.
Your setup
Tutorial, automatic save, left-handed mode and adjustable view height.
Private and offline
No internet connection, account, ads or in-app purchases. The game collects no data.
