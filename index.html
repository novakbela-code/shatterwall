ShatterWall – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-24 · Applies to ShatterWall for Meta Quest, version 1.0 and later

ShatterWall is a virtual reality game for Meta Quest developed by Béla Novák ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game handles. In short: we do not collect, sell or share any personal information. The game keeps its data on your headset and never sends it to us.

Information we collect

None. ShatterWall has no user accounts, no online or multiplayer features, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting. The app does not request the Android internet permission, so it cannot send any data from your headset to us or to anyone else.

Information stored on your headset

To work properly, the game keeps the following data locally on your device:

  • your game settings (for example audio volume and video options),
  • your best Overflow results (round and volley),
  • whether you have finished the tutorial,
  • the run you can continue: its round, number of balls and blocks, and which replay belongs to it,
  • replays of your Overflow runs (see below).

The game never sends this data anywhere, and it does not contain your name or account. It is deleted from your headset when you uninstall the game. If you use a backup feature of the headset, Meta may include app data in it under its own terms; we have no access to such backups.

Replays and movement data

The game automatically records your Overflow runs so that you can watch them again. A replay contains what happened in the game (blocks, balls, effects), the date and time the run started, the game version, and the position and orientation of your head and of your aiming hand (controller) during the run. It contains no camera images, no sound from the microphone, no eye, face or body tracking data and no map of your room.

Movement data is used only to show the replay to you. Replays are stored only as files on your headset; the game does not upload, share or analyse them. The game keeps the current run and the previous one, which newer runs replace, and up to 20 replays that you choose to save. You can delete saved replays in the Replays menu; uninstalling the game deletes all of them. If you copy a replay file or record a video of one yourself, you decide who gets it.

Meta platform

When the game starts, it asks the Meta Quest platform on your headset whether you own a valid license (an "entitlement check"). We only receive a yes/no answer and do not receive or store your Meta user ID, name or any other account information. The purchase, your Meta account and the headset itself are handled by Meta under its own terms and the Meta Privacy Policy.

Children

We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children. Please follow Meta's age requirements for using Meta Quest devices.

Your rights

Because we do not hold any personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. You can delete saved replays in the game, and remove all locally stored game data at any time by uninstalling it. If you have a question or request regarding your data, contact us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of ShatterWall adds features that handle personal information (for example online multiplayer), we will update this policy before that version is released and change the effective date above.
